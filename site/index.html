  1. Tap Open the App Store below.
  2. Tap Get. The Userscripts app is free.
  3. Come back to this page.

Open the App Store

ImportantNeeds iOS 15.1 or later. It’s free.

NextTurn on Userscripts in Safari
Step 2 of 4About 1 min

Turn on Userscripts in Safari

ForiPhone or iPad, using Safari
  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Go to Apps › Safari › Extensions.
  3. Tap Userscripts and switch it on.

ImportantSet All Websites to Allow, or it won’t run on MedBud.

NextInstall MedBud Mobile
Step 3 of 4About 1 min

Install MedBud Mobile

ForiPhone or iPad, using Safari
  1. Tap Open the script below.
  2. Tap the puzzle-piece (or AA) icon in the address bar.
  3. Tap Userscripts, then Install.

Open the script

ImportantOpen it in Safari, not another browser.

NextCheck it worked
Step 4 of 4Seconds

Check it worked

ForiPhone or iPad, using Safari
  1. Tap Check now.
  2. When it says installed, open MedBud.

MedBud Mobile was not found yet. Check the step above, then tap Check now again.

ImportantIf it isn’t found, open the Userscripts menu and switch MedBud Mobile on.
